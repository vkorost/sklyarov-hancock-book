# Sources and Further Reading

## A Note on Method

The methodology behind this book’s cross-corpus analysis is described in the Introduction. None of Sklyarov’s books have been translated into English. All English-language titles below are working translations produced during the extraction process. Russian-language readers can find his complete works through the Laboratory of Alternative History (LAH) archive at lah.ru.

What follows is not a full bibliography. It is a curated reading guide, organized by chapter.
